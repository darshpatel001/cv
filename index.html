01 / PROFILE

About me.

My background, engineering interests and the way I approach technical problems.

Hardware-aware software. System-level thinking.

I am an Electronics & Communication Engineer working in an industrial power-electronics product environment. My interests are centered on firmware development, microcontroller peripherals, industrial communication and connected embedded products.

I enjoy going below high-level abstractions to understand register behavior, data movement, interrupt timing, protocol frames and the interaction between firmware and physical hardware.

Alongside professional experience, I work on practical learning projects involving Ethernet controllers, Linux command-line programming, MCU command interfaces and IoT gateway architecture.
